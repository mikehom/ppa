## Technical Analysis / Mechanism

Example 6.14 [PPA Example 6.14, p. 382] (book p. 382; PDF p. 398) omits the qualifier 'non-trivial' when claiming every strong component in WHILE corresponds to an outermost loop, even though Figure 6.5 on the same page [PPA Figure 6.5, p. 382] (book p. 382; PDF p. 398) contains two trivial acyclic singleton SCCs {x_1} and {x_6}. More critically for performance and widening, flat SCC condensation collapses nested inner while-loops into their enclosing outermost SCC, forcing outer-loop bodies to re-evaluate before inner loops stabilise unless replaced by hierarchical [external: Bourdoncle WTO] [PPA Bibliography [25], p. 441] (book p. 441; PDF p. 454).

Guardrail: Qualify to non-trivial SCCs in L1/L2 and recommend [external: Bourdoncle WTO] [25] when nested loops require inner-first convergence. (Refs: F-U6-07, G-U6-17).
