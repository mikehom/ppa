## Summary

Rewritten [ass] and bind's trailing z:=y omit elementary-block labels, and [call] implies distinct ξ₁ ≠ ξ₂ for val/res formals without explicitly writing ξ₁ ≠ ξ₂.

## Technical Analysis / Mechanism

Two-level SOS (`§2.5.1`) evaluates `[call p(a, z)]^{ℓ_c}_{ℓ_r}` into intermediate term `bind ρ⋆[x ↦ ξ₁, y ↦ ξ₂] in S then z := y`. Neither the trailing copy `z := y` nor the rewritten `[ass]` assignment carries a syntactic `Lab` label—breaking naive invariant proofs that assume every intermediate step has an entry/exit label (deferred by the authors to Chapter 3 closure semantics). In addition, selecting `ξ₁, ξ₂ ∉ dom(ς)` requires `ξ₁ ≠ ξ₂` implicitly so reading `val x` does not alias or corrupt the arbitrary initial value `v ∈ Z` stored at `ξ₂` for `res y`.

Guardrail: Ensure ξ₁ ≠ ξ₂ in fresh parameter location allocators and assign synthetic label on bind continuation if instrumented. (Refs: F-U2b-02, G-U2b-04, G-U2b-05).
