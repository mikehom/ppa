## Technical Analysis / Mechanism

The book relates the interprocedural analysis of §2.5 (call strings) and uniform k-CFA: their abstract caches "have the same overall functionality" and "the two analyses are variations over a theme" [PPA §3.6, p. 196] (book p. 196; PDF p. 214). The book does not compare their costs. [external] In practice the cost of call-string context differs between first-order data flow and higher-order control flow analysis; summaries that present k-CFA as a cheap refinement smooth away the exponential worst-case bound the book states for uniform k-CFA with k > 0.
