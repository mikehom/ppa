## Summary

Book p. 271 writes 'the construction of the reduced product [39] (see Section 4.3.)' and switches from rho[L] to rho_2(L), whereas reduced product is defined in Section 4.4.2 (p. 256).

## Source Citations & Grounding

- [PPA Chapter 4 Concluding Remarks, p. 271] (book p. 271; PDF p. 288)
- [PPA §4.4.2, p. 256] (book p. 256; PDF p. 273)
- Statements confirmed against rendered page images during Unit 4 extraction and Gate #6 audit.

## Technical Analysis / Mechanism

Page 270 denotes the fixed-point image lattice of upper closure operator rho : L -> L with square brackets rho[L] = {rho(l) | l in L} = Fix(rho). Switching to round parentheses rho_2(L) subseteq rho_1(L) on p. 271 conflates lattice images with pointwise element application, and the parenthetical '(see Section 4.3.)' misdirects readers away from §4.4.2 (p. 256) where reduced products are formally constructed.

Guardrail: Route reduced-product cross-references to §4.4.2 (p. 256) and keep square image brackets rho[L] for upper-closure lattices (Refs: F-U4b-08, G-U4b-19).
