## Technical Analysis / Mechanism

Omitted blank versos (book verso pages 364, 416, 428, 438) cause Unit 6 PDF offsets to shift three times: +16 across Chapter 6 [PPA Chapter 6, p. 365] (book p. 365; PDF p. 381), +14 across Subject Index [PPA Index, p. 433] (book p. 433; PDF p. 447), and +13 across Bibliography [PPA Bibliography, p. 439] (book p. 439; PDF p. 452); adding a constant offset yields wrong PDF citations across section boundaries. Furthermore, Concluding Remarks local/elimination solvers [PPA Chapter 6 Concluding Remarks, p. 385] (book p. 385; PDF p. 401) have no Subject Index headwords, while raw OCR drops constraint displays and corrupts '[63]'..'[75]' brackets and 'du-chain' alongside printed quirks ([PPA Index, p. 435] (book p. 435; PDF p. 449), [PPA Bibliography [24], p. 441] (book p. 441; PDF p. 454), [PPA Bibliography [42], p. 442] (book p. 442; PDF p. 455), [PPA Bibliography [90], p. 445] (book p. 445; PDF p. 458), [PPA Bibliography [180], p. 452] (book p. 452; PDF p. 465)).
