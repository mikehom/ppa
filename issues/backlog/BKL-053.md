## Technical Analysis / Mechanism

Reusing `(C'_e, rho'_e)` across Example 3.3 (unacceptable) and Example 3.15 (acceptable table alongside `(C''_e, rho''_e)`) causes bare symbol references to conflate invalid and valid witnesses. In Proposition 3.16, writing `IExp_e` where `Term_e` yields source-only expressions `Exp_e`, and writing `Q_1..Q_n` before fixed `n=4`, obscures the self-application `lfp(Q)` counterexample.

Guardrail: Scope-qualify Example 3.3 vs Example 3.15 candidate names in tests and read IExp_e in Proposition 3.16 as Exp_e. (Refs: F-U3a-06, F-U3a-07, F-U3a-08, G-U3a-15, G-U3a-16).
