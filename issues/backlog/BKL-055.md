## Summary

Table 3.7 counts O(n^3) edge traversals assuming unit-cost add(p_2, D[p_1]) (which costs O(n^4) elementary operations under naive whole-set scans), requires dual registration in E[p_1] and E[p], and computes the least solution of |=_s / C_*[[e_*]] rather than minimal |= on dead closures.

## Technical Analysis / Mechanism

A literal translation of Table 3.7 runs in `O(n^4)` scalar operations whenever `add(p_2, D[p_1])` scans entire closure sets instead of enqueueing individual `(q, t)` pairs with bitsets. Omitting conditional registration from either `E[p_1]` or `E[p]` makes the worklist order-sensitive and drops return/argument flows when `D[p_1]` arrives before `{t} <= D[p]`. Finally, Corollary 3.25 proves soundness `|=` rather than minimality because dead lambda bodies still seed `C_*(l)` under `|=_s`.

Guardrail: Register conditionals in both E[p_1] and E[p], propagate differential (q, t) pairs with O(1) bitset lookups [external], and gate dead lambdas via Mini Project 3.1 R_hat or lazy [9] expansion when needed. (Refs: G-U3a-17, G-U3a-19, G-U3a-24, G-U3a-25, G-U3a-26).
