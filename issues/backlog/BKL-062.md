## Technical Analysis / Mechanism

Treating Exercise 3.4's declarative relation `(3.8)` (`(C_hat, rho_hat) |= e`) as upward-closed under `<=` is false because adding closures to `C_hat(l_1)` triggers previously inactive `[app]` body obligations (`(3.8) => (3.9)` strictly). On pp. 206-209, typographical defects (`"cannot produced"` in Ex 3.3, `in rho_hat(f)` in Ex 3.5, `"semantics allow"` in Ex 3.7, unspaced `f(x)= e` and `"defined in D"` dropping `_*` on `define D_* in e_*` in Ex 3.16, and `"Cartesian Production Algorithm"` in Ex 3.17) plus OCR erasure of displays `(3.8)-(3.9)` confuse automated formula matchers.

Guardrail: Distinguish exact |= ((3.8)) from upward-closed supersets ((3.9)), read "defined in D" in Ex 3.16 as D_*, and transcribe displays (3.8)-(3.9) from PDF page 224. (Refs: F-U3b-10, F-U3b-11, G-U3b-28, G-U3b-30, BKL-011).
