## Summary

Whereas gamma(alpha(l_1) nabla_M alpha(l_2)) induces a valid widening under Proposition 4.44, gamma(alpha(l_1) Delta_M alpha(l_2)) violates (l_1 Delta_L l_2) <= l_1 whenever l_1 notin gamma[M], even on finite Galois insertions.

## Technical Analysis / Mechanism

Proposition 4.44 lifts widening via gamma(alpha(l_1) nabla_M alpha(l_2)) because extensiveness allows passing through the upward closure gamma(alpha(l_1)) >= l_1. By contrast, narrowing must satisfy (l_1 Delta_L l_2) <= l_1 whenever l_2 <= l_1; for any l_1 notin gamma[M], even a finite Galois insertion yields l_1 Delta_L l_1 = gamma(alpha(l_1)) > l_1, refuting contractiveness at the first reflexive pair.

Guardrail: Never induce narrowing via gamma(alpha(l_1) Delta_M alpha(l_2)); use descending truncators Upsilon in L (Exercise 4.11) or clamp with l_1 glb_L (...) under explicit step caps [external] (Refs: G-U4b-18).
