## Summary

Proposition A.10 and the inductive/coinductive definitions of §B.3 assume a monotone functional; §B.3 explicitly rejects a clause such as Q(Con₁(d)) iff ¬Q(d).

## Technical Analysis / Mechanism

Practical policies and rule engines contain negation over recursive facts (taint unless sanitized, declassification exceptions). The induced functional is not monotone, so the least-fixed-point guarantee disappears; solvers may oscillate or return order-dependent results.

Guardrail: L2: forbid negation over predicates in the same recursive group (stratify), or model exceptions as monotone facts; state the precondition next to every fixed-point claim.
