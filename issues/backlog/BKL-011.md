## Technical Analysis / Mechanism

Symbols copied from the text layer produce HALLUCINATED_SYMBOL and SIGNATURE_DISTORTION defects that coordinate validation cannot detect.

Guardrail: Every L3 formula, rule, table and figure is confirmed against a rendered page image (pdftoppm) before admission; extractions record which pages were visually verified.
