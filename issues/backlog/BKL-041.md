## Summary

§2.5 instantiates backward interprocedural analysis with F = flow⋆^R, E = final⋆, IF = inter-flow⋆^R without defining ^R on semicolon flows or 4-tuples.

## Source Citations & Grounding

- [PPA §2.5, p. 85] (book p. 85; PDF p. 104)
- Statements confirmed against rendered page images during Unit 2 extraction.

## Technical Analysis / Mechanism

Forward `§2.5` distinguishes semicolon interprocedural call/return arcs `(ℓ_c; ℓ_n)` and `(ℓ_x; ℓ_r)` in `flow⋆` from matched 4-tuples `(ℓ_c, ℓ_n, ℓ_x, ℓ_r)` in `inter-flow⋆`. Page 85 notes briefly that backward analyses take `F = flow⋆^R`, `E = final⋆`, and `IF = inter-flow⋆^R` without spelling out the reversal rules: orienting semicolon arcs backwards gives `(ℓ_n; ℓ_c)` and `(ℓ_r; ℓ_x)`, while reversing 4-tuples flips both control endpoints and entry/exit roles to `(ℓ_r, ℓ_x, ℓ_n, ℓ_c)` so post-call liveness propagates backward through callee exit `ℓ_x` to call entry `ℓ_c` while restoring pre-return caller facts via `f̂²`.

Guardrail: Define semicolon reversal (ℓ_n; ℓ_c), (ℓ_r; ℓ_x) and 4-tuple orientation (ℓ_r, ℓ_x, ℓ_n, ℓ_c) explicitly for backward passes. (Refs: F-U2b-01, G-U2b-03).
