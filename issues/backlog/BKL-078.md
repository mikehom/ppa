## Summary

Surface §5.4.1 syntax omits dynamic locations xi in Loc produced by new_pi's substitution e_2[x |-> xi], varpi's BNF omits braces around pi, and p. 323 prose calls operator e_1 'the argument e_1'.

## Technical Analysis / Mechanism

In §5.4.1 [PPA §5.4.1, p. 319] (book p. 319; PDF p. 336) [PPA §5.4.1, p. 320] (book p. 320; PDF p. 337), evaluating new_pi x := e_1 in e_2 substitutes a fresh heap address xi in Loc into e_2[x |-> xi], producing runtime terms !xi and xi := e_0 that are ill-formed under the surface grammar restricting dereference/assignment to variables x; a mechanised Subject Reduction proof stalls unless intermediate syntax adds xi in Loc and a store-typing context. Separately, writing unbraced varpi ::= pi | varpi_1 u varpi_2 | empty [PPA §5.4.1, p. 321] (book p. 321; PDF p. 338) conflates singleton elements with sets, misreading p. 323's 'argument e_1' slip [PPA §5.4.1, p. 323] (book p. 323; PDF p. 340) swaps operator and operand roles in Table 5.9 [app] [PPA Table 5.9, p. 322] (book p. 322; PDF p. 339), and relaxing §5.4.1 p. 324 ref_varpi payload invariance [PPA §5.4.1, p. 324] (book p. 324; PDF p. 341) allows writing a subtype-incompatible closure through an aliased reference.

Guardrail: Extend intermediate runtime expressions with xi in Loc, normalise varpi to braced sets, enforce ref_varpi payload invariance, and keep {new pi} unmasked. (Refs: F-U5b-02, F-U5b-03, G-U5b-02, G-U5b-03, G-U5b-04, G-U5b-05, G-U5b-06, BKL-026, BKL-031).
