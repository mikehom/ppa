## Summary

Page 350 line 15 mis-cites Section 5.1 instead of Section 5.4 for shape-conformant subtyping, Mini Project 5.5 leaves 'undefined otherwise' implicit on base addition '+', and PDF OCR erases 21 rules/axioms plus display (5.2).

## Technical Analysis / Mechanism

On p. 350 line 15 [PPA Chapter 5 Concluding Remarks, p. 350] (book p. 350; PDF p. 367), mis-citing Section 5.1 for shape-conformant subtyping misdirects readers to §5.1's subeffecting-only Table 5.2 (which lacks contravariant arrow subtyping introduced in §5.4). In Mini Project 5.5 [PPA Mini Project 5.5, p. 358] (book p. 358; PDF p. 375), omitting an explicit 'undefined otherwise' failure clause on '+' permits unsound addition of two distinct non-none dimension bases (e.g., two timestamps rather than timestamp + duration). In Mini Project 5.4 [PPA Mini Project 5.4, p. 356] (book p. 356; PDF p. 373), Exercise 5.4 [PPA Exercise 5.4, p. 359] (book p. 359; PDF p. 376), and Exercise 5.13 [PPA Exercise 5.13, p. 362] (book p. 362; PDF p. 379), relying on raw PDF text extraction drops display (5.2) and 21 formal rules/axioms across Tables 5.9-5.18, silently truncating type-checker implementations.

Guardrail: Attribute shape-conformant subtyping to Section 5.4, reject adding two non-none bases explicitly, and source all formal rules from visual page-image transcriptions. (Refs: F-U5b-11, F-U5b-12, F-U5b-14, G-U5b-22, G-U5b-23, G-U5b-24, G-U5b-25, G-U5b-26, BKL-011, BKL-030).
