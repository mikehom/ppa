## Summary

Fact 3.11 (p. 160) has its statement erased in ppa.pdf OCR; Tables 3.2-3.3 relabel inner results to outer l in [bind_2], [if_2], and [if_3] so outer labels never change; storing labelled Exp in Val_hat (Exercise 3.6) breaks Fact 3.11 and Theorem 3.10.

## Technical Analysis / Mechanism

Because `[bind_2]` steps `(bind rho1 in v1^{l1})^l -> v1^l` and `[if_2]`/`[if_3]` step `(if true/false^{l0} then t1^{l1} else t2^{l2})^l -> t_i^l`, every SOS transition leaves the outermost label unchanged (p. 157), so `Fact 3.11` (`(C_hat, rho_hat) |= it^{l1} and C_hat(l1) <= C_hat(l2) => (C_hat, rho_hat) |= it^{l2}`) suffices directly for Theorem 3.10 without any `[app_1]` congruence gap. However, storing labelled expressions (`Val_hat = P(Exp)`, Exercise 3.6) breaks `Fact 3.11` when outer labels change across `[bind_2]`/`[if_2,3]`.

Guardrail: Transcribe Fact 3.11 from rendered PDF p. 178, keep outer-label relabelling in [bind_2]/[if_2,3], and store unlabelled Term in Val_hat. (Refs: F-U3a-05, G-U3a-10, G-U3a-12, G-U3b-26, BKL-011).
