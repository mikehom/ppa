## Summary

LV^⊆ on p. 60 suppresses flow^R edges at final(S⋆) without stating isolated exits, causing trailing while-loops and [seq₁]/[seq₂] sub-statements to violate Lemma 2.18 and Theorem 2.21.

## Source Citations & Grounding

- [PPA §2.2.2, p. 60] (book p. 60; PDF p. 79)
- [PPA Lemma 2.18, p. 61] (book p. 61; PDF p. 80)
- [PPA Theorem 2.21, p. 64] (book p. 64; PDF p. 83)
- Statements confirmed against rendered page images during Unit 2 extraction.

## Technical Analysis / Mechanism

Because `p. 60` defines `LV_exit(ℓ) ⊇ ∅` whenever `ℓ ∈ final(S⋆)` (using `⋃{LV_entry(ℓ') | (ℓ', ℓ) ∈ flow^R(S⋆)}` only `otherwise`), any program ending in a loop—such as `S = while [x>0]^1 do [x:=x-y]^2` where `final(S) = {1}` and `(1, 2) ∈ flow(S)`—gives `live_exit(1) = ∅ ⊉ {x, y} = live_entry(2)`. This yields a concrete counterexample to `Lemma 2.18` and breaks `Theorem 2.21(i)` under step `[wh₁]` (`σ₁ = {x↦1, y↦0}` and `σ₂ = {x↦1, y↦5}` satisfy `σ₁ ∼_{N(1)} σ₂`, then diverge on `N(2) = {x, y}`), as well as `[seq₁]`/`[seq₂]` sub-statements whose internal `while` bodies lack isolated exits even when the outer `S⋆` has isolated exits.

Guardrail: Always formulate constraints unconditionally as LV_exit(ℓ) ⊇ ⋃{...} ⊔ ι^ℓ_E (§2.3) in both solvers and proofs. (Refs: F-U2a-09, G-U2a-18, G-U2a-19).
