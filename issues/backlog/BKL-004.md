## Summary

Non-termination is modelled by the absence of a derivation in the natural semantics, so the subject-reduction style correctness result is vacuous for diverging programs.

## Technical Analysis / Mechanism

[external] Reactive or adversarially non-terminating code (event loops, DoS inputs) never yields a final value; effects performed during divergence are not covered by a big-step correctness statement.

Guardrail: L2: when certifying effects of possibly non-terminating code, require a small-step or coinductive argument (SOS of Ch 2, acceptability of Ch 3) rather than the big-step result alone.
