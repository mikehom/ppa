## Summary

For instances of a Bit Vector Framework iterated in the reverse postorder of a depth-first spanning forest `T`, Lemma 6.12 states that the Round Robin algorithm halts after at most `d(G_S, T) + 3` iterations (one for the initialisation step, `d(G_S, T) + 1` propagating passes, and one final stability-detecting pass); Example 6.13 applies it.
