## Summary

Propositions 4.13 and 4.17 inherit f-monotonicity from §4.2 prose (pp. 223, 226) without restating it in their standalone theorem boxes (and Prop 4.17's proof omits citing it); Lemma 4.16 basis (p. 231) prints symbolic n instead of 0; Proposition 4.44(ii) (p. 268) omits n_0 >= 1 when invoking gamma o alpha o gamma = gamma.

## Technical Analysis / Mechanism

Within §4.2, prose on pp. 223 and 226 assumes monotone f : L -> L globally, so standalone boxes of Propositions 4.13 and 4.17 (and 4.17's proof) omit restating f-monotonicity; dropping monotonicity breaks both inductive bounds on f_nabla^m and lfp_nabla^Delta(f). On p. 231, Lemma 4.16 leaves basis step n uninstantiated for n = 0, and on p. 268 Proposition 4.44(ii) applies gamma o alpha o gamma = gamma from n >= n_0 without pinning n_0 >= 1 when l_0 notin gamma[M].

Guardrail: State f-monotonicity explicitly on standalone widening/narrowing claims, instantiate Lemma 4.16's basis at n = 0, and clamp Proposition 4.44(ii) step indices at max(n_0, 1) (Refs: F-U4a-06, F-U4a-08, F-U4b-05, G-U4a-12, G-U4a-15).
