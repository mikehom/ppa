## Summary

Back edges and d(G, T) are defined relative to a DFSF T; Lemma C.12 makes them coincide with dominator-back edges only for reducible graphs; Example C.13 equates d(G) with the maximum loop nesting depth for uniquely labelled WHILE statements.

## Technical Analysis / Mechanism

For Figure C.3, which of the two edges between n₁ and n₂ is a back edge depends on T (though symmetric Figure C.3 happens to have d(G, T) = 1 under both DFSFs, larger irreducible multi-entry graphs have T-dependent d(G, T)). Without unique labels on WHILE blocks, distinct blocks collapse into one node and can break reducibility and the nesting-depth identity. Sibling loops do not add up (Figure C.1 has two loops and d(G) = 1).

Guardrail: L1/L2: always write d(G, T) with its forest, distinguish 'back edge' from 'dominator-back edge', and restrict the nesting-depth reading to reducible graphs (see BKL-010).
