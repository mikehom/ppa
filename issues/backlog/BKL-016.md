## Summary

Dom(n) is defined as the greatest solution of Dom(n) = {n} (n ∈ H), {n} ∪ ⋂{Dom(n′) | (n′, n) ∈ A} otherwise.

## Source Citations & Grounding

- [PPA §C.1, p. 421] (book p. 421; PDF p. 436)
- Statements confirmed against rendered page images during Unit 0 extraction.

## Technical Analysis / Mechanism

Iterating the same equation from ⊥ (empty sets) on a graph with a cycle disjoint from H yields dominator sets that are too small (e.g. ∅ around the cycle), so loops appear to have no dominating header (when every cycle meets H, least and greatest solutions coincide; G-U0-33, G-U0-34).

Guardrail: L2: initialise dominator iteration at ⊤ (all nodes) for non-handle nodes; record the greatest-solution requirement wherever dominators are used.
