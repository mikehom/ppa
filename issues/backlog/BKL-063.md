## Summary

On book p. 269, the 4th line of the '=>' derivation for Display (4.15) prints g(g_{nabla_L}^n) <= g_{nabla_M}^n, applying L-widening subscript nabla_L to g : M -> M instead of nabla_M.

## Source Citations & Grounding

- [PPA Lemma 4.45, p. 269] (book p. 269; PDF p. 286)
- Statements confirmed against rendered page images during Unit 4 extraction and Gate #6 audit.

## Technical Analysis / Mechanism

Inside Lemma 4.45's induction proof, abstract function g = alpha o f o gamma operates on M using iterates g_{nabla_M}^n, whereas nabla_L operates solely on L via f_{nabla_L}^n = gamma(g_{nabla_M}^n) in (4.14). Printing g(g_{nabla_L}^n) on line 4 of the '=>' derivation for (4.15) crosses carriers between L and M even though the next equality substitutes g_{nabla_M}^n and alpha o gamma <= id_M (4.9).

Guardrail: Read line 4 of the '=>' derivation for (4.15) as g(g_{nabla_M}^n) <= g_{nabla_M}^n and enforce gamma(bot_M) = bot_L whenever invoking Lemma 4.45 (Refs: F-U4b-06, G-U4b-17).
