## Summary

With L=𝒫(D), ι ⊆ D so printed ι̂ = {({ι}, ι)} and {(ι, ι)} are ill-typed unless ι is identified with a single d₀; Example 2.41 drops braces and formals u, v and varies y instead of u.

## Technical Analysis / Mechanism

For `L = 𝒫(D)` in `§2.5.5`, the extremal element `ι` is already a subset `ι ⊆ D`. Writing `ι̂ = {({ι}, ι)}` (Large) and `{(ι, ι)}` (Small) commits a set-level type mismatch whenever `|ι| ≠ 1`, because `Δ = 𝒫(D)` expects `(δ ∈ 𝒫(D), d ∈ D)` pairs and `Δ = D` expects `(d₁ ∈ D, d₂ ∈ D)` pairs. Additionally, `Example 2.41` writes 3-component state tuples (`(u, a, ...)` without formal parameters `(x, y)` on Fibonacci's 5-variable store) and varies `y` instead of `u`, obscuring the non-monotonicity witness (`Z₁ = {(δ₀, d₁)} ⊆ Z₂ = {(δ₀, d₁), (δ₀, d₂)}` shrinks `{d'' | (δ, d'') ∈ Z}` output tags).

Guardrail: Use well-typed {(ι, d) | d ∈ ι} (large) and {(d, d) | d ∈ ι} (small), plus monotone ⊆ or small assumption sets (BKL-003). (Refs: F-U2b-06, G-U2b-12, G-U2b-13, BKL-003).
