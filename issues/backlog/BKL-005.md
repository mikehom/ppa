## Summary

The pointer language of §2.6 restricts pointer expressions to p ::= x | x.sel, with one level of selectors drawn from a finite, non-empty set Sel [PPA §2.6, p. 104] (book p. 104; PDF p. 123); the binary arithmetic operations are the standard ones and do not allow pointer arithmetic [PPA §2.6, p. 105] (book p. 105; PDF p. 124).

## Technical Analysis / Mechanism

[external] In C/C++/binaries, offset arithmetic, out-of-bounds writes and unions alias distinct selectors, violating the shape-graph invariants that justify strong updates.

Guardrail: L2: gate strong updates on a prior offset/bounds safety argument; degrade arithmetic-indexed or cast-escaped cells to summary nodes and do not strong-update them ("weak update" is [external] terminology; the book does not use it).
