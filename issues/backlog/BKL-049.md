## Technical Analysis / Mechanism

`Mini Project 2.2` contains three interacting specification defects relative to `§1.5` and `§2.2.2`: (1) It defines `Trace = (Var × Lab)*` without `?`, yet initialises `tr⋆ = ((x₁, ?), ..., (x_n, ?))` (`? ∉ Lab`); (2) It defines `tr ∼ Y` on `Y ⊆ 𝒫(Var⋆ × Lab⋆^?)` with one extra powerset level compared to `Y ∈ 𝒫(Var⋆ × Lab⋆^?)` (`Y ⊆ Var⋆ × Lab⋆^?`); (3) It checks invariant preservation against `Table 2.2`'s `RD^⊆` without demanding isolated entries (which fails when `S⋆` starts with a `while` loop, analogous to `BKL-038`).

Guardrail: Use Trace=(Var×Lab^?)*, Y ⊆ Var⋆×Lab⋆^?, and unconditional RD^⊆ constraints. (Refs: F-U2b-11, G-U2b-30).
